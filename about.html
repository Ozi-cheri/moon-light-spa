About Moon Light Spa

At Moon Light Spa, we are dedicated to providing a sanctuary where you can escape the stress of everyday life. Our spa combines ancient Asian healing traditions with modern wellness techniques, ensuring a truly unique and rejuvenating experience.

Founded in 1995, our journey started in a small Thai village where the art of massage was passed down through generations. Today, we bring that same dedication to well-being to our valued guests in Germany.

Our Values

  • Authenticity: We honor traditional massage methods.
  • Excellence: Our therapists are highly skilled and trained.
  • Tranquility: We create an environment of peace and relaxation.
